Privacy

Disk Heatmap works entirely on your PC. It doesn't have accounts, doesn't collect usage data, and doesn't send anything about your files anywhere.

On this page

  1. What stays on your PC
  2. The one network request
  3. What it changes on your PC

What stays on your PC

  • The scan. File names, folder names, sizes and paths are read and kept in memory on your computer. They are not uploaded or saved to a server.
  • Cleanup advice. The list of known caches is built into the app, so matching your folders against it happens locally too.
  • Your settings, like the theme, are stored on your PC.

There's no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics and no telemetry.

The one network request

When it starts, Disk Heatmap asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. That request fetches a small file describing the latest release from github.com/Anuraj-dev/disk-space-heatmap, and, if there's a new version, downloads its installer from the same place. Like any web request, it reaches GitHub's servers with your IP address; it carries nothing about your files. See Updating.

Show in Explorer, Open and the rest of the app don't use the network.

What it changes on your PC

Scanning only reads. Disk Heatmap deletes files only when you click Clean or Empty bin on a Safe to clean item and confirm. See What Clean does.

This website has no trackers, analytics or cookies, and loads nothing from other sites.
